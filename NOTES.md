# Notes

One suggestion I rejected: a Claude review of this work proposed that the worker poll on a short fixed interval and advance a sheet only once `time.Since(job.UpdatedAt)` reached `STEP_PAUSE`, so that steps stay apart even across a worker restart. I kept one loop that waits `STEP_PAUSE` before every tick and only moved that wait to the top of the loop. Two saves of a sheet are then at least `STEP_PAUSE` apart, after a restart too, without a second interval to tune and without making a new sheet wait a full pause before its first step.

How I checked the result: `go vet ./...` and `go test ./...`, also with `-race` (the new upload tests fail against the earlier handlers, and the type test fails against the original header checks), `npm run build`, and two separate Claude reviews of the whole diff. Then `docker compose up --build` and a scripted pass over the README rules: uploads by type and size with nothing left in the volume, `../../escaped.txt` kept only in `filename`, second and parallel uploads, start without a brochure, repeated and parallel starts keeping one `runId`, 409 for a second running sheet, 404 for another account on every id route, steps saved two seconds apart, `docker compose kill worker` and a restart that finished with the same `runId`, a deleted `body` ending in `Brochure is missing`, the worker waiting for Postgres, and `STEP_PAUSE` parsing. In the browser the open sheet moved through the checklist and showed that error without a reload.
